EN:
16:9, 15-second single take, no cuts or jump cuts, silky zero-stutter camera work, 8K hyper-realistic cinematic look. Dark Chinese opera-house interior: yellow cinnabar talisman papers hanging everywhere, thick red cords strung through hollow gilt miniature copper coins, dark-red woven rush-mat floor; blue-green cold mist + round red light spots, floating dust building Tyndall beams, side-dim lighting, heavy oppressive shadows. Crimson red strings × bright-yellow talismans in violent color clash; extreme detail — crisp hair strands, reflective metal accessories, heavy 3D fabric texture, translucent skin — an eerie, deathly, broken-beautiful Chinese horror puppet mood.

First-person POV opening: fingertips hurl a bronze feng shui compass, needle spinning wildly, gliding out; camera hugs it in a tracking pan. It smashes through a rotten wooden door, tears spiderwebs; passing the door, the camera rises vertically and hovers above the stage, then the compass explodes into golden stardust condensing into a sky of glowing crimson strings. SFX: compass whistle, door creak, web tearing, coin clinks, deep house drone.

Lens micro-pushes then pulls back; the strings sculpt a red-and-black Miao-style girl: top-down shot, she hangs suspended by red threads, arched back, floating down to land — relaxed, unresisting puppet pose. Camera descends to eye-level medium close-up: eyes closed, head up, arms spread, mournful deathly expression; side orbit, she turns slightly, one arm extended, threads and hair drifting.

Push to frontal close-up: eyes open, hand raised to chest, fingertips twirling strings, hazy sinister gaze; canted orbit, ten fingers interlacing and tugging threads in an eerie Nuo dance, headdress tassels swaying; final shoulder-face close-up — desolately gorgeous gloomy eyes, cold faint smile, ethereal frail voice echoing the line: "Red strings pull the puppet; life and death are not hers to choose."

Camera pulls back at constant speed to a full wide: the girl alone at the hall's center, red silk and strings drifting, fractured flowing light. SFX: trembling strings, staccato movement, accessory echoes. A suppressed uncanny Chinese horror BGM throughout.
