# PromptKami #26 — Three Lovers. One Face.

Free English starter adapted from the 36-shot historical-romance workflow. The complete original pack is in Chinese; this is not a full translation.

## Character consistency
Create one original adult male reference portrait. Keep the same face across three roles: a red-robed flirt, a black-clad swordsman, and a shy white-robed scholar. Create a separate adult heroine reference.

## Image prompt
```text
Two original adult East Asian characters in a sunlit historical courtyard, a woman in pale jade-green embroidered robes looking up at a confident man in dark red robes, subtle romantic tension, warm backlight, cinematic realism, natural faces, shallow depth of field, vertical 9:16. Use the same male reference portrait for every costume variant. No text or logos.
```

## Motion prompt
```text
A slow three-second push-in as she studies his face and he smiles. Subtle breathing and natural hair motion. Preserve both faces and their costumes; avoid abrupt movement.
```

## Negative prompt
```text
face drift, extra fingers, deformed hands, plastic skin, watermark, text artifacts, celebrity likeness
```

Generate character references first, then stills, then animate. Results depend on the model and reference controls used.
