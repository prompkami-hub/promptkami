# Second Life, New Choice — Free English Starter

An original short practice exercise inspired by PromptKami Drop #31. This is not the complete private production guide or its translation.

## Story exercise
An adult woman remembers a life she does not want to repeat. She returns to an earlier classroom moment, now age 18, and decides to make a different choice. Show the change through her pause, eye line and a deliberate decision rather than explaining every memory at once. All characters should be adults age 18 or older.

## A starter scene prompt
Cinematic coming-of-age romance with adult characters age 18 or older. A sunlit classroom, an adult woman at a desk, soft window light and a brief look of recognition. Contrast the familiar setting with her newly confident expression. Natural proportions, consistent face and clothing, clear staging, gentle warm color palette. Do not imitate an identifiable real person.

## Three visual beats
1. Establish the classroom and her moment of recognition.
2. Show a quiet reaction that suggests she remembers this encounter.
3. Let a small, visible choice change the direction of the scene.

Create separate character references, keep a shot list and compare continuity before editing. Add any dialogue and captions during editing. Results vary by model and settings; no exact result is guaranteed.
