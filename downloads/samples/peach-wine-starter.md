# Peach blossom wuxia — free English starter

A short creative exercise for PromptKami #17. This is new English starter guidance, not the complete original Chinese 17-shot guide or its translation.

## Establish an original scene
Create an original fictional swordsman in a sunset meadow bordered by peach blossoms. Keep costume, sword, hair and screen direction consistent. Use no real person or named franchise character. Establish the field and distant opponents before cutting closer.

## Build three contrasting beats
1. A clear sword movement sends petals across the frame. Keep the face and silhouette readable.
2. Move to a wider view as a circular energy effect reveals the scene geography. Use petals or motion blur to bridge a cut without hiding continuity mistakes.
3. End on a quiet drinking pose with the sword at rest. Hold the pause so the contrast can land.

## Maintain continuity
Plan matching starting and ending poses. Review hands, prop contact, clothing and the direction of the effects frame by frame. Choose your own short move name if you add a title card; do not copy the source branding or claim a new shoot.

## Finish honestly
Use owned or licensed sound. Add concise English editorial text inside phone-safe margins. Label AI fantasy clearly. Check the opening, middle, ending, full decode and audio peaks before sharing. Do not promise identical model output.
