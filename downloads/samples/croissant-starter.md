# A simple order, a visual surprise — free English starter

A short storytelling exercise for the #10 animation study. This is newly written starter guidance, not the full Chinese nine-shot guide or a promise to reproduce its reference.

## Build an original cast
Choose an original sleepy traveller in a plain blue coat and a friendly orange fox baker with no recognizable brand marks. Keep their silhouette, palette, outfit and relative sizes consistent from shot to shot.

## Set up one clear expectation
Show the traveller imagining an ordinary croissant, walking to a small bakery and placing an order. Use a wide establishing view, a medium two-character counter view and a close-up of the tray. Keep screen direction consistent.

## Pay it off visually
Reveal a harmless fox-shaped pastry on the tray, then cut back to the traveller’s surprised expression. Hold the reveal long enough to read. The joke should work without explaining it in a paragraph.

## Make a short edit
Use one action per shot, simple transitions, short English captions and music or sound effects you own or are licensed to use. Keep names and logos original. A fan reference does not grant rights to reproduce its characters or audio.
