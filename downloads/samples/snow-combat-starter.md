# Snow courtyard action — free English starter

A new short exercise for the #14 AI fantasy reference. This is starter guidance, not the complete original Chinese 15-shot production guide or its translation.

## Keep the scene readable
Use an original fictional fighter in a snow-covered stone courtyard. Choose a simple costume and one red ribbon-like energy effect. Keep the face, outfit, props and screen direction consistent. Do not use a real person or a named franchise character.

## Build three visual beats
1. Establish the courtyard and the fighter in a clear medium shot.
2. Move closer as a hand gesture introduces the red energy. Keep the effect away from the face so the action is readable.
3. Switch to a high overhead view to show the energy spreading across the courtyard. Let snow and architecture maintain the scene geography.

## Connect the movement
Plan the ending pose and camera direction of each shot before generating the next. Use brief dissolves or motion blur only where they help continuity. Review hands, faces, edges and the path of the effect frame by frame.

## Finish an honest short
Add short English editorial captions in safe margins. Use audio you own or are licensed to use. Identify AI fantasy clearly; do not describe the result as real footage. Review the opening, middle, ending and full decode before sharing.
