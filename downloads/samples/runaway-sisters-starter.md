# Runaway Sisters, Unmasked — Free English Starter

A small practice exercise inspired by PromptKami Drop #30, not the complete production guide.

## The scene
Two adult runaway brides visit a lantern festival. They complain about their husbands to two masked strangers. The reveal turns an ordinary conversation into an awkward romantic comedy.

## Practice prompt
Cinematic historical-romance comedy at a lantern-lit night market. Two adult women in distinct pink and lavender embroidered outfits talk to two adult masked men. Keep each character visually distinct. Warm paper lanterns, medium two-shot, expressive but natural reactions, consistent costumes, readable composition.

## A three-beat exercise
1. Establish the two pairs and the festival setting.
2. Cut to a confident complaint, then a silent reaction from the listener.
3. Reveal the identity with a mask removal and hold on the surprised reaction.

Keep separate character references and shot notes. Generate short clips, compare continuity, and edit for timing. Results depend on the model and settings; no exact replication is promised.
