# PromptKami #25 — She Thought It Was a Dream

Free English starter, adapted from the 24-shot historical-romance workflow. The complete original pack is in Chinese; this sample is not a full translation.

## Image prompt
```text
Two original adult East Asian characters in ornate black-and-gold historical robes, a woman gently touching a man's cheek in a softly lit bedchamber, feather-trimmed sleeves, gold hair ornaments, warm amber candlelight, shallow depth of field, cinematic realism, natural skin texture, vertical 9:16, no logos or text.
```

## Motion prompt
```text
Slow gentle push-in. She studies his face as if deciding whether this is a dream. He lowers his gaze, surprised. Subtle breathing and natural hand motion; maintain the same faces and costume details. A three-second continuous take.
```

## Negative prompt
```text
extra fingers, deformed hands, inconsistent face, plastic skin, watermark, text artifacts, celebrity likeness
```

Create character reference portraits before generating shots. Generate a still first, then animate it. Results depend on the model and references used.
