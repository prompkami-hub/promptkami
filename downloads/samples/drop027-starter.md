# PromptKami #27 — He Was Never Harmless

Free English starter inspired by the 17-shot historical-romance workflow. The complete original prompt pack is in Chinese; this starter is not a full translation.

## Character and image direction
Create an original adult historical-romance identity reveal: a white-robed young man lies in a daisy meadow while an adult heroine leans closer. Keep the same original male reference face when he reappears as a black-clad swordsman. Connect the reveal with her surprised close-up. Contrast warm meadow light with cool courtyard light. Vertical 9:16, fictional characters only.

## Motion prompt
Slow three-second push-in on the heroine noticing the white-robed man among daisies. Keep natural breathing and a gentle breeze. Match-cut to his same face in black armor in a courtyard; hold on her surprised reaction. Preserve both faces and costume details.

## Negative prompt
Extra fingers, unstable faces, costume morphing within a shot, text, logos, excessive motion blur.

## Workflow
Lock separate original adult character reference portraits first. Generate each identity scene separately and match color/light in editing. This is AI-generated fiction.
