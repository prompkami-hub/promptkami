# One silhouette, three visual worlds — free English starter

A short starting exercise for the #02 design study. This is newly written sample guidance, not the full 20-shot Chinese guide or a translation of every source label.

## Start with your own character
Describe an original hooded night wanderer with a plain cloak, a simple geometric emblem and a clear silhouette. Keep costume proportions and pose constant across every card; avoid recognizable franchise logos.

## Compare three treatments
Create three square cards in one consistent layout: an antique two-colour print, a bold graphic poster, and a bright pop-art treatment. Change the linework, palette and surface language while keeping the character recognizable. Number each card and use a short medium label underneath.

## Put the cards in motion
Pan evenly across the row. Give each image enough time to read, preserve the entire card and keep captions clear of the artwork. For a vertical edit, fit the wide row inside the frame instead of cropping away neighbouring panels. Add a short English observation about what changed.
