# PromptKami #22 — She Caught Him. With One Ring.

Free English starter. The full original guide is in Chinese, with per-shot generation prompts; this is not a full translation.

## Image direction
Create an original adult princess in pink hanfu playing ring toss in a blossom garden. A golden hoop arcs past the prize jars and lands on the silver coronet of an original adult royal adviser with long silver hair and ivory robes. Cut from the hoop to her stunned reaction, then to a two-shot with her painted silk fan. Warm backlight, consistent faces, vertical 9:16; AI-generated fictional characters only.

## Motion direction
Generate the ring throw, airborne hoop and adviser reaction as separate shots. Match the hoop direction between cuts, keep the same character references and preserve the fan in the final two-shot. Use a slow push-in for the reaction, not a face-changing morph.

## Avoid
Extra fingers, warped hoops, unstable faces, changing costumes, unreadable text and real-person likeness.
