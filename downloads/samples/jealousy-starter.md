# Her Fiancé Saw Everything — Free English Starter

A short practice exercise for a fictional historical-romance misunderstanding. This starter is not the complete 42-shot Chinese production guide and does not promise an identical generated result.

## Before you generate
Create three original adult characters: a woman in pink, her fiancé in black, and a companion in red. Save one clear reference portrait for each. Use the same references, costumes, morning light and street direction across the exercise. Do not imitate a real person or an existing fictional character.

## Three-shot practice sequence
1. **The discovery:** vertical 9:16 close-up of the woman noticing someone outside the frame. Keep the street softly blurred and leave subtitle space below her face. Animate only a small eye movement and a restrained breath, about two seconds.
2. **The response:** matching reverse close-up of her fiancé, in the same light. A controlled expression creates more tension than a large gesture. Keep screen direction consistent, with no face transformation or extra people.
3. **The witness:** cut to the companion watching them. Hold his reaction briefly before cutting back. The joke comes from his uncomfortable presence; avoid explaining the entire situation with captions.

## Assembly checklist
Use clean reaction cuts, consistent character references and readable captions. Check hands, faces, costume continuity and the final vertical crop. Keep music or sound effects original or licensed. Label the finished work accurately as AI-generated fiction. The published example has Chinese dialogue and on-screen captions; this English starter does not add English subtitles to it.
